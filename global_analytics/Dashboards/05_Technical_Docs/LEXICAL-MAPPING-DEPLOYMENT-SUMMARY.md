## KEY METRICS EXPLAINED

### Overlap Percent
**What it is**: How much traditional definition matches Theophysics definition

**Thresholds**:
- <30% = TIER 1 COLLISION (new definition essential)
- 30-60% = TIER 2 BRIDGE (clarification needed)
- >60% = TIER 3 LOW COLLISION (traditional def may work)

**Example**: Coherence = 30% overlap with physics definition

---

### Co-Term Density
**What it is**: How many specialized terms appear within ±15 words of target term

**Interpretation**:
- High density (>200) = embedded in specialized context
- Medium density (100-200) = technical but not extreme
- Low density (<100) = used in general context

**Example**: Coherence = 266 terms per 100 words (VERY high - always in specialized context)

---

### Centrality Score
**What it is**: How central term is in vault graph (0-1 scale)

**Interpretation**:
- >0.7 = Core concept (many links)
- 0.4-0.7 = Important concept
- <0.4 = Peripheral concept

**Example**: Coherence = 0.89 (one of the most central concepts)

---

### DNS (Disambiguation Necessity Score)
**What it is**: Combined metric calculating urgency of new definition

**Formula**: 
```
DNS = (# meanings) × (conceptual distance) × (usage freq) × (1 - overlap%/100)
```

**Thresholds**:
- >5.0 = CRITICAL (new definition essential)
- 2.5-5.0 = HIGH (strongly recommended)
- 1.0-2.5 = MEDIUM (clarification needed)
- <1.0 = LOW (traditional def acceptable)

**Example**: Coherence = 14.28 (CRITICAL - highest priority)
